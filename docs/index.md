![Photo of the Little Luggable sitting on a desk](/images/hero.jpg)

The Little Luggable is my take on a cyberdeck. It's built around the [Raspberry Pi 4 Model B](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-4-model-b/) and the [Pelican 1150 Protector Case](https://www.peli.com/eu/en/product/cases/protector/1150). It includes a fully-custom mechanical keyboard designed to perfectly fit the lid of the 1150.

Little Luggable is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](/license) and I'd love to see people take the design and make it their own. Pull requests are encouraged—I'm excited to see where we can take this. Please share photos if you do make one.
